# **EDITING INSTRUCTIONS** 

**Opening:** Start immediately with your face. No logo animation before the hook. 

**Music:** Low-volume, sophisticated corporate African instrumental. 

**Subtitles:** Always use subtitles because many viewers watch without sound. 

**B-roll:** Keep individual clips between 1–3 seconds. 

**Branding:** Display the ACE Global Nexus logo discreetly in the lower corner. 

**Color:** Use the final ACE Global Nexus brand colors consistently. 

**Pacing:** Confident and deliberate. Do not rush. 

**Voice:** Speak naturally, as though explaining the idea to one serious investor sitting across your desk. 

**Important:** Do not use an AI avatar for this first video. **Your real face and real voice are the foundation of the personal brand.**
