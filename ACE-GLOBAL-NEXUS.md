## About the Founder

In addition, he brings over 13 years of experience in administration and finance management, including expertise in budgeting, operational management, compliance oversight, organizational administration, and strategic planning.

His combined experience positions ACE GLOBAL NEXUS as a trusted and professionally grounded platform for global trade, investment, and strategic business facilitation.

### 1. Executive Summary

#### Company Overview

ACE GLOBAL NEXUS is a global trade, investment, and strategic advisory company headquartered in Yaoundé with a mission to connect businesses, investors, institutions, and entrepreneurs across Africa and the international marketplace.

Founded by Mr. Christopher A. Ekom, a seasoned retired Senior Commercial Specialist from the Embassy of the United States of America in Cameroon with more than 22 years of experience in international trade and investment promotion, as well as over 13 years of experience in administration and financial management, the company leverages deep market knowledge, strategic networks, commercial diplomacy expertise, and strong operational and financial management capabilities to facilitate sustainable global business partnerships.

ACE GLOBAL NEXUS provides:
- International market entry advisory
- Trade and investment facilitation
- Market intelligence and strategic research
- Business matchmaking
- Export promotion and supply chain support
- Global sourcing services
- Diaspora investment advisory
- Corporate training and capacity building
- Strategic business development support

Integrity | Professionalism | Innovation | Transparency | Partnership | Excellence | Sustainability
